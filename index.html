FAQ

What is a CASP?

A crypto-asset service provider is a firm offering crypto-asset services such as custody, exchange, trading platform operation, transfer services, or related services under European regulatory frameworks.

Does MiCA require insurance?

MiCA creates prudential, governance, and safeguarding obligations for CASPs. Whether insurance is relevant depends on the service, jurisdiction, regulatory interpretation, and the firm's risk-transfer strategy. This directory is informational and does not determine regulatory compliance.

How should a CASP choose providers?

Start with the regulated activity, licensing jurisdiction, operational-risk gaps, custody model, financial-crime controls, and evidence needed for supervisors, partners, or insurers. Then validate each provider with counsel and licensed professionals.
