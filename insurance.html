Best approach for EU-regulated CASPs

1. Evidence pack first

Map services, jurisdictions, custody flows, risk controls, incidents, vendors, and governance into a concise insurer-ready evidence pack.

2. Match the right market

Use the directory to understand broker, insurer, custody, and compliance options, but avoid treating every provider as interchangeable.

3. Keep claims conservative

Do not imply MiCA compliance, regulatory approval, or insurance availability until counsel, licensed professionals, and the relevant provider have reviewed the facts.

4. Convert traffic to calls

Insurance-intent visitors should be invited into a short application-readiness review, not a generic sponsor or directory listing form.

CASP insurance FAQ

Does every CASP need insurance?

Not necessarily. Insurance relevance depends on the service, jurisdiction, custody model, counterparty requirements, risk-transfer goals, and the firm's interpretation with counsel and licensed professionals.

What should a CASP prepare before approaching insurance markets?

Corporate and licensing context, activity description, assets and flows, custody setup, security controls, compliance stack, incident history, financials, and any partner or regulator evidence requirements.
